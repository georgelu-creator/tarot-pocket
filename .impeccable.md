# Tarot Pocket design context

## Users and job

Chinese- and English-speaking tarot learners, often on a phone during short breaks
or offline travel. They want to recognize the real RWS image, recall its meaning,
distinguish similar cards, justify an interpretation, and understand how a question
and spread position change the reading. Typing and voice input are inconvenient;
clicking, observing, choosing and recalling should carry the core experience.

面向中英文手机学习者，覆盖碎片时间与无网旅途。核心是记住真实牌面与牌义，
区分相似牌，找到解读依据，并理解问题和牌位的影响。优先点击与静默回忆，
不依赖打字或语音。

## Tone and direction

Quiet, tactile, precise. Warm white and charcoal support sustained reading; plum
marks action and selection. Local serif display headings provide an editorial
rhythm, while system sans-serif keeps questions, options and feedback readable.
Celestial detail belongs on a shared symmetrical card back, not throughout the UI.
Use opaque learning/reading surfaces and sparse frosted floating controls.

安静、有纸牌触感、表达准确。暖白与炭墨用于阅读，梅紫用于动作与选中；
衬线展示标题配系统无衬线正文。天体装饰集中于统一对称牌背，内容不透明，
磨砂材质克制地用于悬浮操作。

## Product invariants

- Keep the Tarot Pocket name. Home has two primary actions: start/continue learning
  and draw. Bottom navigation is Home / Learn / Draw（首页 / 学牌 / 抽牌）; records
  are in the top bar. Foundations and further lessons unfold inside Learn.
- Preserve lessons, difficult but defensible choices, evidence, element/number
  scaffolding, contextual interpretation, stable IDs and existing saved data.
- State wrong answers clearly; selected plum is distinct from success green and
  error red. Meaning must not depend on color alone.
- Use the 78 verified historical faces intact. Reference PNGs are generated
  concepts, not actual screenshots or replacement RWS artwork.
- Keep existing Chinese/English preference and URL behavior. No new daily-draw,
  journal, account, system-language mode or cloud feature follows from a mockup.
- Offline runtime stays self-contained; no font CDN, analytics or image service.
- Motion and material fallbacks preserve operations and the already chosen result.
