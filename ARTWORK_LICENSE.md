# Artwork provenance / 牌图来源

The 78 cards use one historical Rider–Waite–Smith visual edition: the **1910 Pam-A
scans in the Wikimedia Commons TaionWC collection**, illustrated by Pamela Colman Smith.
The individual source pages identify the historical works as public domain.
Tarot Pocket does not claim ownership of the historical artwork or apply its code
or content licenses to it.

Every image has a source page, original filename, dimensions, original and converted
checksums, and an identity review in [assets/manifest.json](assets/manifest.json).
See [assets/SOURCES.md](assets/SOURCES.md), [source evidence](assets/source-evidence.json),
and [visual review records](assets/visual-review.json). Original JPEG scans are
retained alongside derived WebP files; no AI-generated replacements are used.

All 78 images must pass `python3 tools/verify_assets.py` before release. A missing
or mismatched image is a failed build, not permission to substitute another deck.
Preserve the source metadata when redistributing. Public-domain status is recorded
as stated by the source pages; check applicable local rules for your distribution.
