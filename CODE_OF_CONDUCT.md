# Community expectations · 社区约定

Tarot Pocket welcomes learners, teachers, translators, designers, and developers. These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, and other project-managed spaces.

Tarot Pocket 欢迎学习者、教师、译者、设计师与开发者参与。本约定适用于项目 issue、PR 和其他由项目管理的交流空间。

## How we work together · 怎样合作

- Discuss the claim, evidence, or interaction. Do not attack the person. / 讨论观点、依据和交互，不攻击个人。
- Welcome beginners and explain specialist terms. / 欢迎初学者，说明专业术语。
- State the tradition or source behind an interpretation, and allow supported differences. / 说明解读所用体系或来源，容纳有依据的差异。
- Respect identities, backgrounds, boundaries, and privacy. Use invented examples instead of revealing another person's reading or private life. / 尊重身份、背景、边界和隐私，以虚构案例代替公开他人的抽牌或私生活。
- Accept corrections and keep feedback specific and constructive. / 接受纠正，反馈具体且有助改进。

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate exposure of private information, and repeated unwanted contact are not acceptable.

不接受骚扰、威胁、歧视性辱骂、故意泄露私人信息或反复进行对方不愿接受的联系。

## Handling concerns · 如何处理问题

Use the platform's reporting tools for abusive content, or ask a maintainer for a private channel without repeating sensitive details publicly. Maintainers may request an edit, hide or remove content, close a disruptive thread, or restrict participation. The response should match the conduct and its impact.

可使用平台举报工具，或向维护者请求私下沟通渠道，不要在公开回复中重复敏感信息。维护者可要求修改、隐藏或删除内容、关闭扰乱讨论的主题，或限制参与；处理方式应与行为及影响相称。

This is an original, short community agreement. It does not require agreement on every tarot interpretation; it requires respectful, evidence-aware collaboration.

这是一份原创的简明社区约定，不要求大家对每种塔罗解读达成一致，但要求尊重他人并重视依据。
